# Product vision

Learning Compass helps parents nurture children’s curiosity, observation, thinking, creativity, understanding, and inner awareness through everyday experiences. It should feel like a family field journal with the calm of a nature journal and the care of a design studio.

## Who it serves

We are designing for families with children aged 3–9, starting with a parent and a six-year-old. The parent can read or record an entry while the child notices, describes, asks, draws, or leads the next step. A family should be able to use the companion in a short moment at home or outdoors, then return at its own pace.

## The first experience

- **Today** invites the family to notice something nearby, shows open questions and recent discoveries, and offers a simple activity.
- **Observe** captures a dated experience in the family’s own words, with room for sensory details, a surprise, a question, or an image.
- **Wonder** preserves children’s questions and lets the family choose one when the time feels right.
- **Explore** takes a noticing or a question and suggests one invitation. The family can try it, choose another approach, save, or stop whenever they like. The child’s words and what they actually did stay in the journal.
- **Discovery Library** makes earlier observations, questions, explorations, discoveries, photos, and pictures of drawings easy to revisit.

## What success looks like

A child points out something they might have passed by, asks a question in their own words, or changes their mind after looking again. A parent can support that moment without turning it into a lesson or a quiz. Over time, the journal becomes a record of what the family actually did and noticed together.

Use a warm ivory canvas, terracotta accents, sage greens, muted earth tones, and generous whitespace. Keep visual hierarchy calm and controls easy to find on a phone. Choose imagery that draws attention to the real world.
